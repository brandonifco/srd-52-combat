## Ambiguity

Fate: `unresolved`; unresolvedReason: `RequiresInterpretation`.

```text
The tie rule assigns ties among monsters to the GM, among characters to the players, and between a monster and a player character to the GM. It does not say how a tie among characters is ordered when the players do not agree, or who orders a tie between a monster and a character that is not a player character. No measure or set of values is stated for either case and nobody is named, so each is a gap.
```
