## Ambiguity

Fate: `unresolved`; unresolvedReason: `RequiresInterpretation`.

```text
What counts as Difficult Terrain is stated twice, differently. The slice gives only examples (“Low furniture, rubble, undergrowth, steep stairs, snow, and shallow bogs”) and, in “Moving around Other Creatures”, one case (another creature's space). The Rules Glossary's list (difficult-terrain-glossary, p. 181, outside the extent) gives some cases with a measure (“A slope of 20 degrees or more”, “Liquid that’s between shin- and waist-deep”, “A narrow opening sized for a creature one size smaller than you”), qualifies others (“Heavy snow”, where the slice says “snow”; “Furniture that is sized for creatures of your size or larger”, where the slice says “Low furniture”, and the two decide low furniture sized for smaller creatures differently), and ends “or something similar”. Which list governs where they differ, and what else is “similar”, is not stated, and nobody is named to decide, so it is a gap, as surprised is.
```
