```text
Ranges. To determine the range on a grid between two things—whether creatures or objects— count squares from a square adjacent to one of them and stop counting in the space of the other one. Count by the shortest route.
```

## Ambiguity

Fate: `unresolved`; unresolvedReason: `RequiresInterpretation`.

```text
“Count by the shortest route” does not say whether the route may pass through walls and other features that block movement (grid-corners), and for a creature occupying several squares the corpus does not say which adjacent square counting starts from, beyond implying the one giving the shortest count.
```
