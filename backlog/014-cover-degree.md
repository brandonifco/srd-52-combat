## Ambiguity

Fate: `unresolved`; unresolvedReason: `RequiresInterpretation`.

```text
Does “that covers at least half of the target” qualify “Another creature” as well as “an object”? Read one way, any creature between attacker and target gives Half Cover; read the other, only a creature covering at least half of it does. The Cover section's own sentence (“Walls, trees, creatures, and other obstacles can provide cover”) does not choose.
```
